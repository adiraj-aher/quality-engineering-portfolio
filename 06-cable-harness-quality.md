# 06 — Cable & Harness Quality

## Featured qualification work
A reworked harness build was evaluated using a combination of visual inspection, crimp integrity, pull testing and solder-splice inspection.

## Example findings
- Crimp connections remained intact during pull testing.
- Average pull strength was approximately **37 lbf** for the sample reviewed.
- Solder-splice overlap was approximately **0.2 in** in the reviewed sample.
- Acceptance gaps were identified around heat-shrink coverage and adhesive-lined tubing.

## Engineering lesson
A rework process can look visually acceptable while still having workmanship conditions that create long-term reliability risk. Acceptance criteria therefore need to cover the complete joint architecture, material compliance and process controls—not appearance alone.
