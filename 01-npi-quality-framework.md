# 01 — NPI Quality Framework

## Challenge
NPI quality can fail when inspection, process controls and supplier deliverables are created independently. The goal is to create a traceable path from engineering risk to production control.

## Approach
1. Translate product/process risks into PFMEA failure modes.
2. Convert high-risk characteristics into measurable CTQs.
3. Define inspection methods, sampling and reaction plans.
4. Establish MSA/GR&R where measurement risk is meaningful.
5. Use SPC/capability evidence for critical processes.
6. Close PPAP / qualification gaps before production release.

## Deliverables
- PFMEA → Control Plan linkage
- Inspection-plan template
- Measurement-system checklist
- NPI quality gate checklist
- Production-readiness decision matrix

## Engineering impact
This framework emphasizes prevention over end-of-line detection and makes quality evidence auditable across prototype, qualification and mass production.
