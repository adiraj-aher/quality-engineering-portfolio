# 03 — Supplier Quality & SCAR

## Challenge
Supplier escapes require more than sorting. Effective containment must protect production while the supplier proves root cause, corrective action and recurrence prevention.

## Case-study workflow
- Identify failure mode and risk to fleet/customer/production.
- Establish containment and traceability.
- Reference the applicable drawing/spec/IPC requirement.
- Perform supplier RCA / 8D or SCAR.
- Verify corrective action through targeted inspection and testing.
- Confirm effectiveness before disposition or release.

## Featured example
A cable/harness supplier issue involved non-compliant materials/process details and a severe thermal failure concern. The quality response included containment, review of BOM compliance, evaluation of Hi-Pot strategy, rework verification, pull testing and acceptance criteria for heat-shrink/solder-splice conditions.

## Impact
Demonstrates supplier-quality judgment under risk: protect the field and production first, then use evidence to decide whether material can be reworked, accepted or rejected.
