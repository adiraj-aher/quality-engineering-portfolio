# 07 — Reliability Engineering

## Scope
Reliability validation for electromechanical/electronic products using environmental and mechanical stress testing.

## Featured test methods
- Shock
- Vibration
- Thermal/environmental cycling concepts
- Functional verification before/after stress
- Failure-mode documentation and correlation to process controls

## Approach
1. Define the failure modes and risk assumptions.
2. Select stress levels and test duration based on the intended requirement.
3. Capture baseline measurements.
4. Apply stress and monitor failures.
5. Perform post-test inspection and functional verification.
6. Feed findings back into design, supplier and manufacturing controls.
