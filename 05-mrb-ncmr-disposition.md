# 05 — MRB / NCMR Disposition

## Challenge
MRB decisions should be consistent, traceable and technically defensible. Similar defects should not receive different dispositions based on who happens to review them.

## Decision framework
**Defect observed → characterize condition → assess functional/safety risk → determine spec compliance → engineering disposition → rework/replace/scrap/use-as-is → verify effectiveness → release**

## Representative defect modes
- Underfill coverage
- Conformal-coating conditions
- Exposed/insufficiently protected features
- Bent connector pins
- Trace damage
- Stack-up height concerns
- RTV / sealing conditions

## Impact
A structured decision tree improves consistency, reduces unnecessary scrap, and preserves traceability for engineering and quality review.
