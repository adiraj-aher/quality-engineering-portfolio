# Adiraj Aher — Quality & Manufacturing Engineering Portfolio

A curated portfolio of high-impact work spanning NPI quality, supplier quality, PCBA reliability, statistical quality engineering, MRB/NCMR disposition, cable/harness quality, and Six Sigma.

> **Note:** All case studies are sanitized and generalized. No proprietary drawings, customer data, supplier-identifying information, confidential test limits, or controlled company documents are included.

## Core strengths

- **NPI / Manufacturing Quality:** PFMEA, Control Plans, inspection plans, MSA, SPC, PPAP, process qualification, production readiness
- **Electronics Quality:** IPC-A-610, IPC-6012, IPC-7711/7721, IPC/WHMA-A-620; PCBA inspection, rework, reliability and test strategy
- **Supplier Quality:** SCAR/8D, incoming inspection, supplier scorecards, corrective action verification
- **Problem Solving:** RCA, CAPA, DMAIC, Pareto, 5-Why, fishbone, containment and effectiveness checks
- **Data & Analytics:** SQL, Excel, Minitab, Python; yield, AQL, capability, GR&R and trend analysis
