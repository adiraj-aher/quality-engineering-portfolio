# 08 — Six Sigma DMAIC Case Study

## Define
Translate a recurring quality problem into a measurable business and customer requirement.

## Measure
Build a reliable baseline using defect, yield, cycle-time and process data.

## Analyze
Use Pareto, 5-Why, fishbone, process mapping and statistical analysis to isolate dominant causes.

## Improve
Select controls that address the root cause—not only the symptom. Pilot, validate and quantify the improvement.

## Control
Lock the improvement into the Control Plan, work instructions, SPC/inspection strategy and ownership model.

## Portfolio goal
Demonstrate how Six Sigma methods are applied pragmatically in manufacturing rather than treated as a purely academic exercise.
