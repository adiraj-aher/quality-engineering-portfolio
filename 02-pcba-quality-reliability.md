# 02 — PCBA Quality & Reliability

## Challenge
Electronic assemblies require different controls across design transfer, NPI, supplier build and mass production. Inspection alone is not enough; test strategy and reliability evidence must be designed into the quality plan.

## Standards experience
- IPC-A-610 — electronic assembly acceptability
- IPC-6012 — rigid printed board performance/specification framework
- IPC-7711/7721 — PCB/assembly rework, modification and repair practices
- IPC/WHMA-A-620 — cable and wire harness acceptance

## Featured engineering work
- Developed and executed IPC-based inspection criteria across NPI and production stages.
- Contributed to design and qualification of ICT, including a robust strategy for switching between JTAG and boundary/alternate access paths.
- Structured PCBA reliability testing covering shock, vibration and thermal environments.

## Impact
The focus is on qualification evidence that catches failure modes before field deployment and provides clear acceptance criteria for production.
